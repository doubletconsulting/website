Services

Support across the full disaster cycle.

From preparedness and planning to response and long-term recovery, I bring command-level experience to every phase.

Incident Management & IMT Deployment

Experienced command and general staff support when an incident outgrows day-to-day capacity.

  • Incident Management Team deployment and augmentation
  • Command and general staff positions (ICS)
  • Emergency Operations Center (EOC) activation and support
  • Incident Action Planning and operational-period briefings
  • Transition planning from response to recovery

FEMA Public & Individual Assistance

Program support to help jurisdictions navigate FEMA PA and IA and maximize eligible recovery funding.

  • Public Assistance project formulation and documentation support
  • Grants Portal navigation and applicant briefing preparation
  • Individual Assistance coordination and survivor-program support
  • Damage assessment coordination
  • Eligibility and documentation reviews to reduce de-obligation risk

Disaster Recovery Operations & Applicant Advocacy

On-the-ground recovery leadership that keeps survivors, elected officials, and partners moving in the same direction.

  • Recovery organization design and management
  • Applicant advocacy with state and federal partners
  • Multi-agency coordination and stakeholder engagement
  • Long-term recovery planning support
  • Lessons-learned and after-action reporting

Emergency Management Planning

Plans that hold up when it counts, written to be used, not shelved.

  • Emergency Operations Plans (EOPs) and annexes
  • Continuity of Operations / Continuity of Government (COOP/COG)
  • Hazard mitigation plan development and updates
  • Preparedness assessments and gap analysis
  • Plan alignment with NIMS, CPG 101, and state requirements

HSEEP Exercises, Training & Instruction

Exercises and training built to the Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program standard.

  • Discussion-based exercises: seminars, workshops, and tabletops
  • Operations-based exercises: drills, functional, and full-scale
  • Exercise evaluation and controller/evaluator support
  • After-Action Reports and Improvement Plans (AAR/IP)
  • Leadership and ICS-focused training and instruction

Crisis Management, Business Continuity & Resilience

Helping organizations stay operational and credible through disruption.

  • Crisis management team structure and playbooks
  • Business continuity planning and impact analysis
  • Executive crisis-decision workshops
  • Crisis communications coordination
  • Organizational resilience assessments, including cyber-incident readiness

Public-Safety Advisory

Senior-level counsel on operations, policy, and technology adoption from someone who has run the agency.

  • Operational and organizational assessments
  • Policy development and criminal-justice reform strategy
  • Public-safety technology evaluation and adoption planning
  • Multi-agency communications and interoperability strategy
  • Executive coaching for new and aspiring public-safety leaders
